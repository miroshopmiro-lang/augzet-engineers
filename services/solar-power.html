Your roof is a power plant

When energy is coming from the sun, why are we digging the earth? Customized rooftop and ground-mount solar plants with 3D drawings and software-based simulations, so the energy generation and cost savings you sign off on are the ones your meter delivers.

Simulation before installation

Most installers quote from a satellite photo. We model. Every Augzet solar project begins with a physical site survey, shading analysis and load-profile study, converted into a software simulation and 3D drawings of the finished plant.

The result is an energy forecast you can hold us to (daily generation, seasonal variation, payback timeline) before a single panel is ordered.

  • Site survey, shading study and structural assessment
  • Software-based generation simulation with 3D plant drawings
  • Internationally certified panels, inverters and BOS components
  • Grid synchronization, net-metering and MNRE subsidy processing
  • Complete documentation handover for future maintenance and upgrades
  • Annual maintenance plan options from day one

Subsidy handled, not just promised

Our clients have received MNRE subsidies in as little as 40 days after commissioning, because the drawing set, documentation and registration are prepared as part of the project, not as an afterthought.

Common Questions

Before you ask

How large a plant does my roof need?

That comes out of the load study, not a price list. We look at your actual consumption pattern, the usable shade-free roof area and your orientation, then size the plant against what you use rather than what fits.

How long does the MNRE subsidy take?

Our fastest was credited 40 days after commissioning. That is not a guarantee (processing times vary), but it is achievable because we prepare the drawing set, documentation and registration as part of the project rather than afterwards.

What happens if the generation is lower than the forecast?

That is exactly why we simulate rather than estimate, and why you get the model before you sign. The forecast covers daily generation and seasonal variation, and the monitoring at handover lets you check the plant against it yourself.

Do you handle net-metering with KSEB?

Yes. Grid synchronization and net-metering are part of the package, along with the documentation each stage requires.

What maintenance does a solar plant actually need?

Panel cleaning, performance checks against the expected curve, inverter health and connection integrity. All of it is covered under our AMC plans, serviced by the team that installed the plant.
