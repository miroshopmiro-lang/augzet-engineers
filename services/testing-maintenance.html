How It Runs

What a visit actually covers

01

Inspection & testing

Visual inspection of boards and terminations, thermal and load checks, earthing integrity and verification that protective devices are still doing their job.

02

Performance check

For solar, output measured against the expected generation curve, plus panel condition, cleaning and inverter health. For automation, diagnostics and updates.

03

Report & rectification

You get a written record of what was found and what was done, added to the documentation set from the original install, so the system's history stays in one place.

04

Breakdown response

Priority response between scheduled visits, from engineers who already have your drawings and know what is behind the wall.

Coverage

Common Questions

Before you ask

Will you maintain a system somebody else installed?

Yes. We start with an inspection to document what is actually there, which is often the most useful part of the first year, because many systems arrive with no drawings at all.

How often are the visits?

It depends on the plan and the system. A domestic electrical installation and a commercial solar plant do not need the same frequency, so we scope the schedule to the system rather than selling a fixed package.

What do the plans cost?

Plans run from a basic annual inspection to comprehensive cover, priced against the size and type of system. We quote after seeing it, transparently, with no hidden charges, which is the phrase our reviews repeat most.

What counts as a breakdown call?

Anything that has stopped working or looks unsafe. Priority response is included in the plans, and because we hold your documentation we usually arrive knowing where to look.

Can one contract cover electrical, solar and automation together?

Yes, that is the point of a single point of contact. One contract, one team, no arguments about whose fault it is.
