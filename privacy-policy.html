Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 August 2026.

Who we are

This policy covers augzet.com, run by Augzet Engineers, Door No: 105, Royal Way Building, Muttom, Near Metro Pillar No: 182, Ernakulam, Kerala 683106. For any privacy question, write to info@augzet.com or call 0484-2684066.

What we collect

The only personal information this website collects is what you type into the quote form on the Contact page: your name, phone number, email, location, project type, approximate monthly electricity bill, and any project details you add. We do not use tracking cookies and this site does not set any.

How the quote form works

The quote form does not submit to a server. When you press send, it opens a pre-filled email in your own email app, addressed to info@augzet.com, so the enquiry goes directly to our inbox as a normal email. We never receive or store anything unless you actually send that email.

Why we collect it

Solely to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote, and get in touch about your project. We do not use it for anything else, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Who else sees it

Nobody, by default — the form goes straight to our inbox as an email you send yourself. This site is hosted with a standard web host and loads Google Fonts for typography; loading a font may share your IP address with Google in the ordinary way that any font request does. We do not run analytics or advertising scripts on this site.

Uploads

This website does not accept file uploads. If you send us drawings, photos or documents, it will be as an email attachment or over WhatsApp, and it is stored the same way any business email or chat attachment is.

How long we keep it

Enquiry emails are kept as part of our normal business correspondence for as long as needed to handle your project and for our own record-keeping. You can ask us to delete your details at any time — see below.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email info@augzet.com and we'll action it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of this page will change too.
