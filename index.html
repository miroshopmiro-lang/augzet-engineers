Our Mission

Advanced electrical. Advanced solar. One system.

Technology moves fast, and the trade has split with it. The firms doing advanced electrical work aren't focused on solar, and the solar firms aren't electrical engineers. Augzet exists to close that gap: when new solar technology arrives, we integrate it into the electrical system that is already there and hand the result over as one solution.

Licensed for the work

Augzet holds the government licences required for the electrical work it undertakes, held in-house rather than borrowed for a job.

An in-house engineering team

Design, drawings and simulation are produced by Augzet's own engineering team, not outsourced to a consultant between projects.

Technicians on staff

Installation and maintenance are carried out by technicians on Augzet's own staff, so the people who commission a system are the people who come back to service it.

Our Approach

Designed on paper. Built to last.

Every job at Augzet starts as a documented design: a load study, a sized cable, a coordinated protection scheme, a single-line diagram. It's the standard behind our 1,000+ kW of solar, our automation installs and every electrical system we build. Same rigor, same drawings, same engineers.

Most contracting firms treat engineering as a formality on the way to installation. We do it the other way around, and it shows in reviews that keep repeating the same words: planned, organized, on time, no hidden charges.

Flagship Delivery

Design it. Build it. Keep it running.

An 80 kWp on-grid solar plant for M/s. Sunny and Sunnys Business Ventures Pvt Ltd, Ernakulam, and the three-stage process behind every job we deliver.

01 / Electrical Design

The drawing comes first.

Load study, cable sizing, protection coordination, earthing, single-line diagrams: all signed off before a single panel or cable is ordered. For this plant that meant a physical site survey and shading study, converted into a software simulation and 3D drawings of the finished array, not a quote off a satellite photo.

02 / Engineering & Installation

Certified products. Documented work.

Certified components, engineered mounting, labelled and documented wiring. Our clients tell the same story in their reviews: no hidden charges, well planned, executed inside the promised time frame.

03 / Handover & Aftercare

One contact, for the life of it.

We handed over a working plant with its full drawing set, registered the MNRE subsidy (credited in 40 days), and put the system on an annual maintenance plan serviced by the engineers who built it.
